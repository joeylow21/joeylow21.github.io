Joey Low is an independent scholar of History, specializing in the China-Vietnam borderlands and Eurasian comparative history. He holds a BA from CSU Bakersfield, a MA from CSU Fullerton, and a PhD from Brandeis. He also served as a visiting graduate student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His research focuses  on borderland studies, legal studies, and economic and fiscal history in early modern polities of Eurasia, and he is currently revising his dissertation into a book.
